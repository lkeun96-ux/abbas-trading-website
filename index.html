Logistics

From our yard in Gimpo to your port.

Gimpo sits between Incheon Port and Incheon Airport, so consolidation and loading happen close to where your shipment leaves Korea. We book the freight, clear the export and send you the paperwork — you receive one set of documents from one company.

How it works
01

Send your request

A part number, a photo, or the model and year. WhatsApp or email, in Korean, English or Arabic.

02

Quote with real photos

We check the shelf, photograph the exact item and quote the price including packing and freight.

03

Payment and packing

Once payment clears we pack in-house and load the container at our own yard in Gimpo.

04

Shipping and documents

Export declaration, B/L and invoice go out to you, with tracking until the shipment arrives.
